Spotted a mistake, something unclear, or an idea for a new article? The best place for it is GitHub.

The guide is maintained in public. Open an issue for corrections, questions or suggestions. Short and specific is perfect: which page, what is wrong, and what you would expect instead.

Open an issue See the GitHub organisation

Good things to raise

  • An error of fact, or advice that no longer holds for current tools.
  • A section that did not make sense on first reading.
  • A broken link or a page that renders badly on your device.
  • A topic you would like an article on.

Please do not include private code, credentials or anything confidential in an issue. Issues are public.
